# Wikipedia

Save Wikipedia articles from your phone or computer for offline reading.

- Search articles or paste a Wikipedia link in the reader's File Manager.
- Read a clean, single-column EPUB with section navigation and simplified layout.
- Optimized grayscale images, stacked infobox facts, and narrow-screen tables.
- Articles saved in `/Wikipedia`, with source, revision, and image credits.
- Optional InterWiki font installation for pronunciation and common scientific symbols.
- No account, hosted backend, or firmware modification required.

Enable plugins and open **File Transfer > Join Network** on the reader.
Scan its URL QR code on your phone, open File Manager, and find Wikipedia.
The phone and reader must share a network; the phone also needs internet.

The browser performs article conversion. Reader font settings apply;
enable Embedded Style to use the included article stylesheet.

Source: https://github.com/saadasif/crosspoint-wikipedia
